## Web

The web team manages and maintains the official websites and online platforms for Rocky Linux. This includes the building and maintaining of tools and interfaces that the community uses, and that the Web and other teams uses for administration.

## Design

The design team handles the project's visual identity, including branding, logos, and other artistic artifacts. This includes logos, banners, and digital graphics for websites, social media, and other project platforms.

## Contact

You can reach the web team at [their Mattermost channel here](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/web). You can reach the design team at [their Mattermost channel here](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/design).
