These resources are common to the Infrastructure team.

## Account services

Where you create and manage your Rocky Linux account and sign agreements.

* URL: [Account services](https://accounts.rockylinux.org)
* Technology: Noggin used by Fedora Infrastructure.
* Contact: [Infrastructure in Mattermost](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/infrastructure) and `#rockylinux-infra` in Libera IRC.

## RESF Git service

General projects, coded, and more for the Rocky Enterprise Software Foundation (RESF).

* URL: [Git service](https://git.resf.org)
* Technology: [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org)
* Contact: [Infrastructure](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/infrastructure) or [Development](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/development) in Mattermost, and `#rockylinux-infra` or `#rockylinux-devel` in Libera IRC.

## Rocky Linux GitLab

Packages and light code for the Rocky Linux distribution.

* URL: [Rocky GitLab](https://git.rockylinux.org)
* Technology: [GitLab](https://gitlab.com)
* Contact: [Infrastructure](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/infrastructure) or [Development](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/development) in Mattermost, and `#rockylinux-infra` or `#rockylinux-devel` in Libera IRC.

## Mirror services

Application for users to become a mirror host for Rocky content, either for the operating system or SIG content.

* URL: [Mirror services](https://mirrors.rockylinux.org)
* Technology: MirrorManager 2
* Contact: [Infrastructure](https://chat.rockylinux.org/rocky-linux/channels/infrastructure) in Mattermost, and `#rockylinux-infra` in Libera IRC.
