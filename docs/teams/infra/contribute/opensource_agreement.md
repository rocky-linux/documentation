However in this case, this agreement is also exactly what a user must agree to in Account services. For this reason, you should make no changes to the wording of this document unless they are also made to the agreement in Account services. The only changes made during migration were to formatting, while leaving the wording as is. The one exception was to remove references to the wiki, which will be archived once the team completes migrations.
